# Autotest Review Checklist\n\nSemantic: linked requirement, correct branch, meaningful assertions.\nFalse positive: swallowed errors, weak/non-empty assertions, setup failure, wrong object.\nAsync: bounded polling, no arbitrary sleep, unique IDs.\nIsolation: independent data, cleanup, no order dependency.\nMocks: do not hide behavior under test.\nMaintainability: reuse helpers, avoid duplicate fixtures and implementation-detail assertions.\n
